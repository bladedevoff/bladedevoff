## Hi, I'm BladeDev 👋

ML engineer who ships the whole thing: the model, the backend around it, the infra it runs on
and the security review before it goes out.

- 🧠 **ML:** distilling big LLMs into small local heads, GPU pipelines, benchmarks I can defend
- 🧩 **Backend and fullstack:** proxies, APIs and the UI on top of them
- ⚙️ **DevOps:** CI, trusted publishing to PyPI, GPU boxes from a laptop to an AMD MI300X
- 🛡️ **Security, blue team:** hardening and monitoring, plus redaction and code and dependency scanning in every release

- **[stuntd](https://github.com/bladedevoff/stuntd):** a local proxy that learns your LLM's typed decisions and answers the confident ones with a small head, in about 20 ms instead of a model call. On [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/stuntd/), with a [Hugging Face Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollix/stuntd).
- **[studiomi300](https://github.com/bladedevoff/studiomi300):** one prompt to a 30-second reel, with a director agent, a vision critic and image, video, music and voice models on a single AMD Instinct MI300X. Built solo for the AMD Developer Hackathon, May 2026: finalist, winner of the Hugging Face track and the Qwen Special Prize.
